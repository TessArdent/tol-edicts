# 1.0.7c Patch Notes

- Date: 2017-10-31 23:46 UTC
- Author: Boslof
- Feed: Community Announcements
- Original: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/2140760316584262889

## General:

- Added ability to undo in journal with Ctrl+Z

- 1 Neutral Killer is guaranteed to spawn in 16 player games.

- Increased the spawn rate of healers in the game. (Phys/Alch)

## Mastermind:

- Reworded "In The Shadows" to specify investigative abilities instead of classes.

## Hunter:

- Spike Traps renamed to Bear Companion. Now instantly kills players attempting to attack you. Will not kill any other players. (Killing of players trying to convert the Hunter may be added later.)

## Psychic:

- Misdirect can now be used on Reaped players.

## Bug Fixes:

- Fixed issues with the Hunter's Bear Companion.
