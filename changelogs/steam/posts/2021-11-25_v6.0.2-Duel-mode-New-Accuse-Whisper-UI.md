- New game mode "Duel mode":

Duel Mode (7-9 players):
Selected roles will be assigned from the Blue Dragon, the Unseen or the Cult, excluding Neutral faction.

Unseen/Cult are capped at 2 members.
[Possible Role]

- All Blue Dragon.

- Mastermind, Assassin.

- All Cult.
