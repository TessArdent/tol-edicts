**PATCH NOTES:**
Added sounds for different messages sent by players.
Added sounds for being attacked and being healed at night.
Added a sound for when the Psychic links minds with you.
Added an option to disable the fps display in the settings.
Added shortcuts for the journal, deathnote, and private notes. They can be changed in the launcher.
J opens Journal by default.
D opens Deathnote.
N opens Private Notes.
Escape can be used to close most of the UI popups.
Improved login screen stability
Updated privacy policy (more emphasis regarding chat logs storage from our new report system)
Updated terms of service (more clarity regarding drawing obscenities on the deathnote)

**HEADS UP:**
DEATH NOTES HAVE BEEN TEMPORARILY DISABLED UNTIL WE CAN GET A REPORT SYSTEM IMPLEMENTED FOR THEM.
Added in a word filter for messages, since people can't say racist things. For the time being the messages just won't send. It filters mostly racial slurs and a few extreme insults.

Most of these improvements were from community feedback!
