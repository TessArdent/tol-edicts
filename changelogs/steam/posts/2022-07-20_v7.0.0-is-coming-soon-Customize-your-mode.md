# v7.0.0 is coming soon: Customize your mode!

- Date: 2022-07-20 03:19 UTC
- Author: UnZan
- Feed: Community Announcements
- Original: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4598755821860019152

---

Greetings, citizens!

We are delighted to announce that we are adding a brand new feature - Custom Mode to the game!

This mode is designed for 7-16 players and the host can customize the roles they want for each player in the game. (I.e When the game started with 12 players, the host can customize the player role from Player #12 to #1 for the game.)
![](https://clan.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/images/27884844/bc11942ea4e57aaf16e8d31a11d6299fc4aa4ce0.png))

If you’d like to use a specific role for the players, simply ignore the “Random” option and choose the desired role.
![](https://clan.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/images/27884844/bc11942ea4e57aaf16e8d31a11d6299fc4aa4ce0.png)

If you want to avoid the rat’s best friend joining, simply unclick the “Prince” role from the Random Pool.
![](https://clan.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/images/27884844/87c12285e8663e536a8f10c3a6e06c1702a0dd93.png)

Imagine how dangerous would it be for Sheriff or Paladin in the game?
![](https://clan.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/images/27884844/569404e3c20b4d16c350bcc653164f6c13ae88c2.png)

The Custom Mode will become live on July 27th and we will shut down the server temporarily for the update from 12am to 4am (EST).

We’d also like to share the great news of the updated Roadmap:

- Extra Custom Mode options (I.e Customizing evil’s maximum, partial random pool, saving the setting, etc)
- More items will be added to the Store for you to purchase.

Last but not least, we will change the game to a “pay-to-play” game in the future. This will not affect the players who already downloaded the game. We'd also like to thank all the support from our beloved citizens, even though we still have many fun ideas like to be seen in the game, but without your support, none of that would come true. We will drive to keep maintaining the game for better quality for all citizens.
