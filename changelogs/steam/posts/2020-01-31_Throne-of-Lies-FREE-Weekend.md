*Throne of Lies: The Online Game of Deceit*'s **FREE Weekend** is now live!

Gather your friends to attempt to claim the Throne for yourself! Befriend who you betray in this online social deduction game of wit. New and veteran players may login this weekend (Friday+) to claim **2,000gp** for a limited time to celebrate the Prince's birthday event!

### **1st:** Learn *how* to take the Throne!

- [Join our Discord](https://discord.gg/tol) and say hi to the 10's of thousands of players within👋 Ask questions live 24/7 in #ask-a-guide

- When you login, ensure completion of the "core mechanics" tutorial

- Before you dive in, press **TAB** to learn a few classes. TAB works in-game, too! Especially note: 1) Prince, 2) Mastermind, 3) Assassin, 4) Sheriff, 5) Blue Dragon / Unseen Kings

- Note the "Glossary" and "Guidebook": You can press ESC to access these beyond the lobby.

- Prefer tons and tons of data? We have a community Wikia @ https://tol.wikia.com

- You will have a <Newcomer> title above your head: Don't worry, most players will cut you some slack and attempt to guide you!

- Noticed toxicity beyond an acceptable "PG-13" level? Be sure to report them at the end of the game (to the right of their name)! We **heavily moderate**: Be good ;)
We hope you enjoy this free weekend on us. We Devs (+Volunteer Mods, Judges, and Guides) hang around the Discord all the time: Hop in if you have any questions! We're gamers just like you. We want to make sure you have an enjoyable experience~

Thanks for being part of this celebration! *Throne*! :)
