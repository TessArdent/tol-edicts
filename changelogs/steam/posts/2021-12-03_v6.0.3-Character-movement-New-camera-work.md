v6.0.3: Character movement, New camera work.

- (**New) Character movement.

- (**New) Camera view control.

- Updated "Play Now" button rule.

- New Christmas scene.

Detail :

- Character movement.

- You can control your character to move around in day scene.

- Use mouse click or W,A,S,D keys to control the character movement.

Have fun with the new movement, and emote to interact with others!

![](https://clan.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/images/27884844/7df80c6ff70c78a4e9d0b611cda50a56f89cfcd7.jpg)

- Camera view control.

- Use right click or J,L keys to control camera rotation.

- Use mouse wheel or I,K keys to control camera zoom.

- New camera movement when start the trial!

Enjoy with the new Immersive experience about new camera view!

![](https://clan.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/images/27884844/75f6a18026fad4ac47a0aac407a47a31aeb4530a.jpg)

- Updated "Play Now" button rule.

Click "Play Now" to find the room with the most people in ALL MODE.

- New Christmas scene.

A new BIG ARMORSNOWMAN and a big present in Christmas scene.
Put on your Christmas suit and enjoy it!
