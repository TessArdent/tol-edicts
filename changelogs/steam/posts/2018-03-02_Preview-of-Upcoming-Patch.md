It's been a while since our last patch - for a good reason.

Throne of Lies has officially been available for five months, and tens of thousands of you have joined us at the round table.

We thought it was time to spruce up the user interface and to include new ways for players to express their individuality.

Along with some balance changes, we will also be including a new game mode called Casual Mode.
It's a simplified version of the current classic game mode. It will have less classes and will only feature the Unseen, but all the classes included will be the same between the two game modes.

The patch will be released in a couple weeks after a private and public testing period.

Stay tuned for a peak behind the curtain at a more colorful kingdom where all of the visions of the players behind castle Adiart will become more vivid and alive.... until you die.
