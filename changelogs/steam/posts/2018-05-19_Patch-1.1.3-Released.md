# Patch 1.1.3 Released

- Date: 2018-05-19 14:42 UTC
- Author: Ellizira
- Feed: Community Announcements
- Original: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/2391853754543025128

Along with some smaller balance changes, the Mercenary has been reworked, and the Sellsword has been revived as a Neutral Offensive.

An MVP system has been added to the game, where you can vote for players that made a big impact.

See the full patch notes in-game or on our [forums](https://forum.imperium42.com/t/1-1-3-patch-notes/63029)
