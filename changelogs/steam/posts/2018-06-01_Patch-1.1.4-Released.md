# Patch 1.1.4 Released

- Date: 2018-06-01 14:56 UTC
- Author: Ellizira
- Feed: Community Announcements
- Original: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/2396358622001129008

---

When you purchase gold, the amount in each tier is now doubled.
Everyone who has previously bought gold, will receive the extra gold they would have gotten in the new system.

There are also some minor balance changes this patch, such as the Mystic and Sellsword both being unique now.

See the full patch notes in-game or on our [forums](https://forum.imperium42.com/t/1-1-4-patch-notes/65388)
