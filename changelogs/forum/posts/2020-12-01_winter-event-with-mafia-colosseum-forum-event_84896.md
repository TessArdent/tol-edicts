# **Happy Holidays!**

To celebrate another year of Throne of Lies, as well as the upcoming holidays, we’ve worked together with [**Mafia Colosseum**](https://www.mafiacolosseum.com/threads/welcome-to-the-winter-event.500) to create some fun games and activities for you all to enjoy.

## Forum Games

- **[@osieorb18](https://fol-archive.netlify.app/u/osieorb18)’s Micro League** - [**SIGNUPS**](https://www.mafiacolosseum.com/threads/the-cyberiad-mafia-micro-mini-league-signups-1-14.499/)

  *Trurl and Klapaucius are two genius robot constructors who travel around the universe aiding those in need. This 14-player anonymous mafia league is the story of the Seven (Nine) major Sallies into Space of Trurl and Klapaucius.*

  - Hosted on Mafia Colosseum
  - 14 players
  - Anonymous
  - Closed setups
  - Phases are 48/24
  - Starts as soon as it fills!
- **[@DatBird](https://fol-archive.netlify.app/u/datbird)’s JoJo’s Part 7 Forum Mafia - [SIGNUPS](https://www.mafiacolosseum.com/threads/jojos-bizarre-adventure-part-7-forum-mafia-signups-6-19.498/)**

  *A rolemadness game based on Part 7 of the fan favorite manga/anime JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure*

  - Hosted on Mafia Colosseum
  - 19 players
  - Closed setup
  - Phases are 48/24
  - Starts December 14th
- **[@Arete](https://fol-archive.netlify.app/u/arete)’s JoatJoatJoat13**

  JoaTJoaTJoaT13 is an open set-up where every power role is a JoaT. Since it has 3 JoaTs, it’s 1.5x better than JoaT^2.

  - Hosted on Forum of Lies
  - 13 players
  - Open setup
  - Phases are 48/24
  - Starts December 21st
