- There are TONS of Throne of Lies YouTube videos and Twitch streams about us! [Look us up](https://gaming.youtube.com/results?search_query=throne%20of%20lies)!

- We’re working ways to load between day/night faster. **WAY faster.**

Oh my, a lot of good things lately and on the way.
