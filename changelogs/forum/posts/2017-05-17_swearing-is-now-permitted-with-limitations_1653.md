> Swearing is permitted. However, keep it classy – don’t overdo it and no targeting people. Some BOTs will automod “heavy swear words”. Feel free to “word around” the filter (to censor your words). No racial slurs, etc.

**Rule #1** was recently updated for several reasons:

1. Swearing used as an adjective (99% of the use) should not offend anyone. It simply supercharges the word. For example, “*fcking awesome!*”
2. We mods and devs swear. We’re bad examples and we apologize for being hypocritical.

a reminder: **Just because you CAN swear, doesn’t mean you should**. Overdoing it or using it as personal/directed insults (f YOU) will still result in disciplinary action. **Keep it classy.** The Discord BOT will still automod f words and racial slurs. However, minor swearing was removed from automod and the forum won’t be modded for swearing **unless it’s directed or crossing lines, like racial slurs.**
