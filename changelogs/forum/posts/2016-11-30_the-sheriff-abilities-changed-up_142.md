We changed [The Sheriff’s](http://tol.wikia.com/wiki/The_Sheriff) ability today - The former ability:

~~**Day Ability 1 (Reveal Mustache):**
**[1 attempt power]** The Sheriff can make the system of the game reveal himself as The Sheriff. Other players will know that you are The Sheriff~~

is now removed. The mustache has been shaved (in spirit). *The new ability is at night:*

**Night Ability 2 (Posse Comitatus):**
[1x] Recruit a fellow Blue Dragon to become a [Knight](http://tol.wikia.com/wiki/The_Knight) class. Cannot convert Royalty or Unique classes. Reveals the Sheriff to the target, even if unable to convert.
