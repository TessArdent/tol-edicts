## General:

- Apostle can now spawn with the Cult Leader.
- Whispers are disabled on the first day.

## Minor Class/Ability Changes:

> #### Mercenary:
>
> - Now wins if their contract wins.
> - No longer commit suicide if their target wasn’t a Neutral class.
> - Cannot use abilities once contract has died.
>
> #### Sorcerer:
>
> - Can now use Circle of Death before night 3.

## Major Class/Ability Changes:

> #### Noble:
>
> - Snoop removed.
> - Maid Spy now reveals all whispers the next day, but is now limited to 3 uses.
> - Added new night ability Gossip - See the feedback of abilities used on target player. Will not receive feedback for conversion or attacks. Unlimited uses.
>
> #### Aristocrat:
>
> - Snoop removed.
> - Maid Spy now reveals all whispers the next day, but is now limited to 3 uses.
> - Added new night ability Intrigue - Frame a player and see the feedback of abilities used on target player. Will not receive feedback for conversion or attacks. Unlimited uses.
>
> #### Apostle:
>
> - Snoop removed.
> - Cult Spy now reveals all whispers the next day, but is now limited to 2 uses.
> - Added new ability Mind Warp - Cause a player to receive incorrect feedback for their ability when possible. 3 uses.
>
> #### Marshal:
>
> - Added Snoop. 2 uses.

## Lobby

> - Fixed the start/queue buttons from showing in public match so trolly people won’t get you caught in an infinite “Finding Group” screen :wink:
