I just discovered some new info, wondering why [Patreon’s Thronepass](https://forum.imperium42.com/t/new-thronepass-patreon-community-perks-forum-name-colors-titles-flairs/76833) would sometimes fail to link. There are several ways to link between FoL and Patreon:

## How to Link Patreon<>FoL

- If you registered with the Patreon button, you’re ready.
- If you linked your Patreon “Associated Account” in your profile settings area in the social linking area, you’re ready.
- If you enabled 2FA, [there seems to be a bug](https://meta.discourse.org/t/how-do-i-enable-associated-accounts-with-2fa/129490/7) where you can no longer “link” accounts, so you’ll need to ensure **your email must match** between Patreon<>FoL.
