**[The Knight](https://tol.wikia.com/wiki/The_Knight)** (Wikia)

The Knight class is an “Offensive” type for the Blue Dragon faction.

This class is both offensive and defensive, with abilities to either defend (by taking your own life for theirs), or to kill another in cold blood.
